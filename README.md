# HealthConnect Clinic – Patient Appointment Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

HealthConnect Clinic is a fictional healthcare provider that manages appointment-based services.

This project analyzes the HealthConnect Appointment Dataset to understand patient appointment attendance, identify patterns associated with missed appointments, and generate insights that can support better appointment management and patient support.

### Business Question

**How can HealthConnect Clinic use data to reduce missed appointments and improve the patient support experience?**


## 🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Understand appointment attendance and no-show patterns.
- Identify factors associated with missed appointments.
- Analyze patient and appointment characteristics.
- Examine previous appointment history and previous no-shows.
- Evaluate the effect of reminders on appointment outcomes.
- Analyze waiting time and distance to the clinic.
- Examine cancellations and appointment outcomes.
- Develop relevant healthcare KPIs.
- Generate actionable insights and recommendations.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dataset is fictional and anonymized.
- The analysis is limited to the variables available in the dataset.
- Missing or inconsistent records may affect some results.
- The analysis identifies relationships and patterns but does not establish causation.
- Some reasons for missed appointments may not be captured in the available data.
